# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming regardless of identity, background or experience. Discuss technical decisions with evidence; respect privacy and differing viewpoints. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and project-related interactions. Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate contributions and warn, temporarily restrict or ban participants proportionately. Maintainers are subject to the same standards.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile contact options; for sensitive security/privacy incidents use a private security advisory. Do not publish identifying details of affected people. Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible, with fair consideration and protection against retaliation.
